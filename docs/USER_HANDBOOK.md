# Member guide

SST Alumni Connect is the official network of SST Public School Rashidabad graduates and students.

## Join
1. Open the website and choose **Join the network**.
2. Enter your name, email, mobile number and a password, then your SST details (admission and graduation year,
   class, registration number if you know it) and where you live and work today.
3. Confirm your email address with the link we send you.
4. The alumni office verifies every member — usually within a few days. You can sign in meanwhile; some areas
   (directory, community, messages) open once you are verified. Speed it up: complete your profile, or attach a
   document (e.g. a school certificate) under **My profile → Verification**. Documents stay private.

## Your profile and privacy
- **My profile**: photo, biography, current job, experience, education, skills, achievements and any additional
  details the school asks for.
- **Privacy**: choose who sees each part — all members, only your connections, or only you. Your email and mobile
  number are private unless you choose otherwise.
- **Account settings → Security**: change your password, turn on two-factor authentication, see and sign out
  other devices.
- **Account settings → Your data**: download a copy of all your data, or ask for your account to be deleted.
- Language: choose **English** or **اردو** from the top bar; the layout switches to right-to-left for Urdu.

## Find people and stay in touch
- **Directory**: search by name, batch, profession, city or country; send connection requests; follow people.
- **Messages**: private chats and groups, with photos and files, read receipts and typing indicators.
- **Community**: the feed, your batch group and discussion groups. Report anything inappropriate.
- **Notifications**: the bell shows what happened; choose in **Notifications → Preferences** what you are told by
  email.

## Events
Open **Events**, choose an event and **Register** (bring guests if allowed). Your ticket with a QR code is on the
event page — show it at the entrance. If the event is full you join the waitlist and are told when a place opens.

## Jobs and mentorship
- **Jobs**: browse and save jobs, apply with your CV through the portal, and track applications. Post jobs or offer
  referrals for fellow alumni — the career office reviews them first.
- **Mentorship**: find a mentor by industry and expertise and send a request, or apply to become a mentor.

## Giving back
**Donations & giving**: choose a campaign or a general donation, pledge an amount, then pay by bank transfer, cash or
cheque using your reference number. When the office confirms the payment you receive a receipt by email and can
print it from **My donations**. You can give anonymously.

## Help
Use **Contact** on the website or email the alumni office. Forgot your password? Use **Forgot password?** on the
sign-in page.
